# Rapid Response Plumbing & Restoration: Style Guide (locked 2026-10-24)

Source of truth: `src/styles/tokens.css`. Brand source art: `brand-source/`.

## Brand
A high-energy emergency brand with a motorsport feel: fire vs. water, chrome, a dark base, and forward-leaning italic type.
Keep the site cleaner than the van wrap: dark sections, bold type, one glow color at a time.
The fire/water art is only for the hero and CTA bands.

## Colors
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| --color-fire | #E3202B | Primary CTAs, "Restoration" accent |
| --color-flame | #FF7A1A | Highlights, gradient |
| --color-water | #1E8BFF | Secondary CTAs, links, "Plumbing" accent |
| --color-chrome | #C9CED6 | Outlines, phone, sub-text on dark |
| --color-black | #05070D | Utility bar, dark sections |
| --color-navy / -2 | #0A1A3F / #10275A | Nav bar, dark surfaces |
| --color-mold | #2E9E3E | Mold icon only |

Signature: `--gradient-fire-water` (the 3px line under the nav) and `--gradient-chrome` (text on dark).

## Type
- Headlines, phone numbers and the call button: **Saira**, ExtraBold 800, *italic*, width 112.5
- Body, nav, forms and buttons: **Barlow**, 400–700

## Buttons
Pill shape. Call = fire red with glow. Secondary = water blue with glow.
