Water damage in a business is a disruption. Water damage in your home is personal. It's where your family sleeps and eats, and where the things you can't replace are kept. Rapid Response provides **residential water damage restoration in Victorville** and across the High Desert 24/7, with a crew at your door in 1 hour or less. We handle the drying, the leak and the paperwork so you can focus on your family.

### What homeowners worry about most

When we walk into a wet house, the questions are almost always the same: *Is it safe? Can we stay here? What's ruined? Will insurance pay for this?* We answer them on the first visit. You'll know which rooms are affected, what has to come out, how long drying should take and what to expect from your claim before we start the equipment.

### Your homeowners insurance claim

Most homeowners only file a water damage claim once or twice in their lives. We work with insurance adjusters every week. On a typical home job we:

- **Photograph the damage** before anything is moved or removed
- **Record moisture readings** in every affected room, every day until it's dry
- **Document the cause**, which is often the deciding factor in whether a loss is covered
- **Send a written scope** of the mitigation work to you and your adjuster

We don't decide what your policy covers, but good documentation is the best way to make sure the claim reflects what actually happened. For more on what a full restoration job includes, see [water damage restoration](/water-damage-restoration/).

### Rentals, landlords and tenants

Rental homes are common across Victorville, Hesperia and Adelanto, and water damage there involves two people: the owner and the tenant. We keep both in the loop, work around the tenant's schedule and give the owner the documentation for their claim. Landlords with several properties can call us directly for every unit.

### Manufactured homes in the High Desert

Manufactured and mobile homes are a big part of the housing in the High Desert, and they get water damage differently than a house on a slab. Supply lines run under the floor, so a leak can fill the belly wrap and soak the insulation before anything shows inside. Particleboard subfloors swell and break down when they stay wet. We check under the home as well as inside, and dry or replace what's needed.

### Common household leaks

Most home water damage starts with something small: a [washer hose or dishwasher](/appliance-leak-cleanup/), a toilet supply line, a [failing water heater](/water-heater-leak-cleanup/) or a slab leak. Carpet is often the first thing affected. Our [carpet water damage](/carpet-water-damage/) page covers how we decide what can be saved. Because we're also a plumbing company, we fix the leak during the same visit.
