When water gets loose in a home or business, the clock starts. Within minutes it runs under flooring and into wall cavities. Within a day or two, wet materials can start to grow mold. **Water mitigation** is the work that stops that spread: extracting the water, removing what cannot be saved and drying the structure. Rapid Response provides water mitigation and structural drying in Victorville and across the High Desert 24/7.

### Mitigation vs. restoration

You will hear both terms from contractors and insurance adjusters. **Mitigation** is the emergency response that keeps the damage from getting worse. **Restoration** is the full process of returning the property to its pre-loss condition, including repairs. Insurance companies often handle the two separately, and most policies expect the property owner to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, which is exactly what mitigation does. See our [water damage restoration](/water-damage-restoration/) page for the full process.

### What structural drying really means

Drying a building is not the same as drying a floor. Water soaks into carpet pad, subfloor, framing, drywall and insulation, and those materials can stay wet long after the surface feels dry. Structural drying uses:

- **Moisture meters** to find water you cannot see
- **Air movers** placed to move air across wet surfaces and into wall cavities
- **Dehumidifiers** to pull moisture out of the air so materials keep releasing it
- **Daily monitoring** and adjustments until readings show the structure is dry
