Most hot water heater leaks are found by accident. Someone steps onto a wet hallway carpet, opens the garage and sees a dark line on the slab, or notices a baseboard that has started to swell. By then the water has usually traveled further than it looks. Rapid Response provides **water heater leak cleanup in Victorville** and the rest of the High Desert 24/7, and we repair or replace the heater on the same visit.

### Slow leak or sudden failure: the cleanup is different

A **sudden failure** puts a lot of clean water on the floor at once. Speed matters most here. The faster we extract it, the more carpet, laminate and drywall can be dried in place instead of torn out.

A **slow leak** is the opposite. There may only be a small puddle, but water has been soaking into the bottom plate of the wall and the subfloor for weeks. That calls for moisture mapping, removing the materials that have stayed wet too long, and checking for mold before anything gets closed back up.

We'll tell you which situation you have after the first round of readings.

### What we check after a water heater leak

- **The base of every wall** next to the heater, inside the wall cavity and on the other side of it
- **Carpet pad and tack strip** in hallways and the bedrooms next to the closet
- **Subfloor under laminate and vinyl plank**, where water stays trapped under a floor that looks dry
- **Cabinet bases** when the heater shares a laundry room or utility space
- **The garage slab and anything stored on it**, especially cardboard and particleboard shelving

### Why hard water speeds up leaks here

Much of the High Desert has hard water. Mineral sediment builds up in the bottom of the tank, the metal there runs hotter, and the tank corrodes from the inside. That's why so many of the leaks we clean up come from tanks around 8 to 12 years old. If your heater is in that range and has started rumbling, our [water heater repair and replacement](/plumbing/water-heaters/) team can check it before it fails.
