Water damage does not wait. Once water gets loose inside a home or business, it keeps moving, soaking into flooring, wicking up drywall and dripping into the spaces below. Rapid Response provides **water damage restoration in Victorville** and across the High Desert 24/7, with crews on the way in 1 hour or less.

### What water damage restoration includes

Restoration is more than drying a wet floor. A complete water damage restoration job includes:

- **Finding and stopping the source** of the water
- **Emergency water extraction** from floors, carpet and lower levels
- **Moisture mapping** with meters to find water hidden in walls and under flooring
- **Selective removal** of drywall, flooring, trim and insulation that cannot be saved
- **Structural drying** with air movers and dehumidifiers until materials reach dry standards
- **Documentation** with photos and moisture readings for your insurance claim

### Why drying it right matters

Surfaces can feel dry long before the materials underneath are. Drywall, subfloor and framing that stay damp can warp, smell and grow mold weeks later. We track moisture throughout the drying process and do not call a job dry until the readings say it is.

### The advantage of a plumbing and restoration company

Most water damage starts with plumbing: a burst pipe, a failed water heater, a slab leak. With most companies, you need a plumber for the leak and a separate restoration crew for the damage.
