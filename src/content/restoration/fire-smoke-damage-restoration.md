A fire is one of the most stressful things a property owner can go through, and the damage it leaves behind is complicated. There is the fire damage itself, smoke and soot throughout the structure, and the water used to put the fire out. Rapid Response provides **fire damage restoration in Victorville** and across the High Desert, handling the cleanup of all three so your property can move toward recovery.

### Smoke and soot travel farther than you think

Smoke moves through every open space in a building, including walls, attics and HVAC ducts. It leaves soot residue and odor on surfaces, contents and fabrics well away from where the fire burned. That is why fire cleanup has to cover the whole affected area, not just the burned room. Soot is also oily and acidic, so trying to wipe it off with household cleaners can smear it and set the stain.

### The water damage nobody expects

When firefighters put out a fire, they use a lot of water, and sprinkler systems add more. That water soaks into carpet, subfloor, drywall and insulation. Left wet, those materials can warp and grow mold. Because Rapid Response specializes in [water damage restoration](/water-damage-restoration/), we extract and dry firefighting water as part of every fire job. If mold has already started, see our [mold remediation](/mold-remediation/) service.

### What fire damage restoration includes

- **Assessment** of fire, smoke and water damage throughout the property
- **Stabilization** to protect the property from further damage
- **Removal** of burned, charred and unsalvageable materials
- **Smoke and soot cleanup** on affected surfaces
- **Water extraction and drying** after firefighting
- **Documentation** with photos and notes for your insurance claim
