  - q: My carpet looks dry but smells musty. What's wrong?

Wet carpet is one of the most common things we see after a leak, and one of the most misunderstood. Many people assume it has to be torn out. Others run a rental carpet cleaner over it and hope it dries. Neither is usually the right call. Rapid Response provides **carpet water damage cleanup in Victorville** and across the High Desert 24/7, and we start by finding out whether your carpet can be saved.

### Save or replace: how we decide

Three things decide whether wet carpet can stay:

1. **What the water was.** Clean water from a supply line or water heater gives the best odds. Grey water from an appliance drain needs cleaning and sanitizing. Carpet soaked by sewage or outside flood water is removed. See [sewage cleanup](/sewage-cleanup/) for that work.
2. **How long it has been wet.** The first day or two matters most. After that, delamination and mold become much more likely.
3. **The condition of the carpet.** Older carpet with worn backing, or carpet that has already bubbled or separated, often isn't worth drying.

We walk you through what we find, and we document the decision for your adjuster either way.

### Carpet over slab

Most High Desert homes have carpet laid over a concrete slab. Concrete holds moisture, and a wet pad pressed against it dries very slowly. That's why we often lift the carpet, remove the soaked pad and dry the carpet and the slab separately. Leaving the pad in place over a damp slab is how a small leak turns into an odor problem weeks later.

### The dry desert air helps, but it's not enough

Low outdoor humidity in the High Desert helps materials release moisture, but a closed-up house with the AC or heat running doesn't dry carpet and pad on its own. Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers control the drying, and moisture readings show when the job is done. Our [water extraction](/water-extraction/) crew handles the first step, pulling as much water as possible out of the carpet and pad before drying starts.

### Where the water came from matters

Carpet damage almost always starts somewhere else: a [washing machine or dishwasher leak](/appliance-leak-cleanup/), a failed water heater, a burst pipe or a toilet overflow. Because we're a plumbing company too, we fix the source on the same visit. If the carpet was wet long enough for mold to start, our [mold remediation](/mold-remediation/) team takes care of it.
