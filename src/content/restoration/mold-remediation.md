Many people assume mold is not a problem in the desert. But mold does not need a humid climate. It needs moisture, and leaks, slab leaks and water damage that was never fully dried give it exactly that. Rapid Response provides **mold remediation in Victorville** and across the High Desert, and because we are also a plumbing company, we fix the moisture source so the mold does not come back.

### Why mold shows up after water damage

Drywall, insulation, wood framing and carpet pad all hold moisture. When they stay wet for more than a day or two, mold can begin to grow, often out of sight inside walls, under flooring or behind cabinets. By the time you see spots or smell a musty odor, the growth has usually been there a while. That is why fast, thorough [water damage restoration](/water-damage-restoration/) is the best mold prevention there is.

### What professional mold remediation includes

Remediation is more than wiping a surface. A proper job includes:

- **Inspection** of visible mold and likely hidden growth nearby
- **Containment** to help keep spores from spreading to clean areas
- **Selective demolition** of drywall, insulation and materials that cannot be cleaned
- **Cleaning** of remaining surfaces in the affected area
- **Moisture control** so the conditions that grew the mold are gone

### Fix the source or it comes back

The most important step in mold remediation is the one many companies skip: fixing the water problem. A pinhole leak in a wall, a slow drain leak under a sink or a [slab leak](/plumbing/slab-leak-repair/) will keep feeding mold no matter how well the surface is cleaned. Our plumbers find and repair the source with [leak detection](/plumbing/leak-detection/), then our restoration team handles the cleanup.
