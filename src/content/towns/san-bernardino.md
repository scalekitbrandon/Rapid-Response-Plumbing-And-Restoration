San Bernardino sits at the foot of the San Bernardino Mountains, where I-215 and I-10 meet the Cajon Pass route up to the High Desert. It is the county seat and one of the largest cities in the Inland Empire, with older established neighborhoods, foothill homes, Cal State San Bernardino and plenty of commercial property. Rapid Response provides **plumbing and restoration in San Bernardino**, on call 24/7.

### Older homes, older plumbing

Many San Bernardino homes have been standing for decades, and so have their pipes. Galvanized steel and older copper lines corrode, narrow and start leaking, and older clay and cast iron sewer lines crack and fill with roots. We handle [leak detection](/plumbing/leak-detection/), [burst pipe repair](/plumbing/burst-pipe-repair/), [sewer line repair](/plumbing/sewer-line-repair/) and [repiping](/plumbing/repiping/), and because we are also a restoration company, we dry and restore the water damage those failures leave behind.

### Fire season and smoke damage

San Bernardino's foothills sit in the path of fire season and Santa Ana winds. Even homes far from the flames can end up with smoke and soot inside. Our [fire and smoke damage](/fire-smoke-damage-restoration/) team cleans up smoke and soot and handles the water left behind from firefighting.
