Daggett is a small, unincorporated desert community just east of Barstow, along historic Route 66 and near the Barstow-Daggett Airport. ### Rural plumbing problems

Exposed pipes, long supply lines and plumbing under manufactured homes are all vulnerable to freezing nights and wear. A leak in a buried line may only show up as wet ground or a well pump that runs too often, and a leak under a manufactured home can soak the subfloor and insulation long before anyone notices. Our [leak detection](/plumbing/leak-detection/), [burst pipe repair](/plumbing/burst-pipe-repair/) and [plumbing repairs](/plumbing/plumbing-repairs/) services find and fix the problem.
