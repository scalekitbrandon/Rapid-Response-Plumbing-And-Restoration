Hesperia stretches along I-15 just south of Victorville, climbing toward the top of the Cajon Pass. That extra elevation means colder winter nights than much of the Victor Valley, and when storms roll through, runoff moves fast. Rapid Response provides **plumbing and restoration in Hesperia** 24/7 for homes, rentals and businesses across the city.

### Cold nights and burst pipes

When temperatures drop below freezing, pipes on exterior walls, in garages and at hose bibs can freeze and crack. The leak often does not show until the pipe thaws, and by then water is running inside the wall. Our [emergency plumbers](/plumbing/emergency-plumber/) repair the break and our restoration team extracts the water and dries the structure before it turns into a [mold](/mold-remediation/) problem.

### Storms and runoff

Hesperia does not get much rain, but monsoon storms can drop a lot of it quickly. Runoff can cross lots and push water under doors and into garages. Our [flood cleanup](/flood-damage/) team extracts water, removes materials that cannot be saved and dries what can.

### Drains, sewer lines and slab leaks

Clogged main lines and [slab leaks](/plumbing/slab-leak-repair/) are two of the most common plumbing calls we get in Hesperia. We clear backed-up lines with our [drain cleaning](/plumbing/drain-cleaning/) service and, if wastewater reached your floors, handle the cleanup and sanitizing too.
