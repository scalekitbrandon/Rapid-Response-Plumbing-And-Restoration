Henderson is the second-largest city in Nevada, stretching from established Green Valley neighborhoods to newer master-planned communities like Inspirada and Anthem in the foothills. Rapid Response provides **plumbing and restoration in Henderson**, on call 24/7 for homes, condos, rentals and businesses.

### Hard water, slab homes and hot garages

Henderson shares the Las Vegas Valley's very hard water, and nearly every home sits on a concrete slab. That combination makes [slab leaks](/las-vegas/slab-leak-repair/) and early [water heater](/las-vegas/water-heaters/) failures some of our most common calls. Most water heaters sit in garages that get extremely hot in summer, which adds wear.

### Older neighborhoods, older pipes

Much of Green Valley and other established Henderson neighborhoods were built during the 1980s and 1990s, when polybutylene supply pipe was common. If you are seeing leak after leak, ask about [repiping](/las-vegas/repiping/).

### One team for the leak and the damage

When water gets loose, our [emergency plumbers](/las-vegas/emergency-plumber/) repair the source and our restoration team handles [water mitigation and structural drying](/las-vegas/water-mitigation/) and [water damage restoration](/las-vegas/water-damage-restoration/). If moisture has led to [mold](/las-vegas/mold-remediation/), we handle that too.

### The AC factor

Henderson air conditioners run for months and produce a lot of condensate. A clogged line or cracked drain pan can drip into a ceiling or closet for weeks. That, slow slab leaks and water heater leaks are the most common mold sources we see in Henderson. We contain the area, remove affected materials, clean and dry, and repair the source.

### Desert air is not enough

It is easy to assume everything dries fast in Henderson. Surfaces do, but water under tile, in the slab and inside walls stays put in a closed, air-conditioned home. We extract, remove what cannot be saved, and run dehumidifiers and air movers until moisture readings confirm the structure is dry.

### Condos, townhomes and shared walls

In Henderson condos and townhomes, a leak in one unit often reaches the next. We map and dry every affected unit and document it for owners, HOAs and insurers, so everyone is working from the same record.
