Henderson is the second-largest city in Nevada, stretching from established Green Valley neighborhoods to newer master-planned communities like Inspirada and Anthem in the foothills. Rapid Response provides **plumbing and restoration in Henderson**, on call 24/7 for homes, condos, rentals and businesses.

### Hard water, slab homes and hot garages

Henderson shares the Las Vegas Valley's very hard water, and nearly every home sits on a concrete slab. That combination makes [slab leaks](/las-vegas/slab-leak-repair/) and early [water heater](/las-vegas/water-heaters/) failures some of our most common calls. Most water heaters sit in garages that get extremely hot in summer, which adds wear.

### Older neighborhoods, older pipes

Much of Green Valley and other established Henderson neighborhoods were built during the 1980s and 1990s, when polybutylene supply pipe was common. If you are seeing leak after leak, ask about [repiping](/las-vegas/repiping/).

### One team for the leak and the damage

When water gets loose, our [emergency plumbers](/henderson/emergency-plumber/) repair the source and our restoration team handles [water mitigation and structural drying](/henderson/water-mitigation/) and [water damage restoration](/henderson/water-damage-restoration/). If moisture has led to [mold](/henderson/mold-remediation/), we handle that too.
