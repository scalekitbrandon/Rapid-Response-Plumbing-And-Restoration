Phelan is a rural, unincorporated community west of Hesperia, spread out along Highway 138 and Phelan Road at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains. Homes here sit on large parcels, many with barns, shops and long runs of pipe. Rapid Response provides **plumbing and restoration in Phelan** and nearby Piñon Hills 24/7, and one call covers both the repair and the cleanup.

### Built for mountain-edge weather

Phelan is one of the highest communities we serve, and its winters are colder and snowier than anywhere on the valley floor. Pipes running through unheated garages, crawl spaces and outbuildings are the first to freeze. When they thaw, a crack you never saw can run water into walls and under flooring for hours. Our [emergency plumbers](/plumbing/emergency-plumber/) stop and repair the leak, then our [water mitigation and structural drying](/water-mitigation/) crew extracts the water and dries the house.

### Water heaters in a cold climate

Colder water coming into the house means the water heater runs longer and harder all winter. Sediment builds up, relief valves start to drip and older tanks finally give out, often in a garage or utility closet where nobody sees the leak right away. We handle [water heater repair and replacement](/plumbing/water-heaters/) and dry out any damage the old tank left behind.

### Septic, roofs and wind

Most Phelan properties are on septic rather than a sewer main. A tank that needs pumping or a cracked line can back wastewater into the home, and that calls for [sewage cleanup](/sewage-cleanup/), not a mop. The wind that sweeps down off the mountains also drives rain under roofing and around vents. Water that gets into an attic soaks insulation and stains ceilings, and if it sits, it can turn into a [mold](/mold-remediation/) problem.

### Fire season

Brush covers much of the open land around Phelan, and in 2016 the Blue Cut Fire forced evacuations across the community. When fire or smoke reaches a home, our [fire and smoke damage](/fire-smoke-damage-restoration/) team removes soot, deals with lingering odor and cleans up the water left behind by firefighting.
