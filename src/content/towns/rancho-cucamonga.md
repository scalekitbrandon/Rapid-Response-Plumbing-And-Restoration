Rancho Cucamonga sits at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains on the west end of the Inland Empire, with master-planned neighborhoods like Etiwanda and Victoria, shopping at Victoria Gardens and a lot of larger two-story homes. Rapid Response provides **plumbing and restoration in Rancho Cucamonga**, on call 24/7.

### Leaks from upstairs

In two-story homes, a leak in an upstairs bathroom or laundry room does not stay upstairs. Water runs through the floor and comes through the ceiling below, soaking insulation and drywall on the way down. We find and fix the source with [leak detection](/plumbing/leak-detection/) and [plumbing repairs](/plumbing/plumbing-repairs/), then handle [water mitigation and structural drying](/water-mitigation/) so the ceiling and walls dry out properly.

### Slab leaks and water heaters

Many Rancho Cucamonga homes are built on concrete slabs, where a leaking line can go unnoticed for weeks. [Slab leak repair](/plumbing/slab-leak-repair/) and [water heater](/plumbing/water-heaters/) service are two of our most common plumbing calls, and we restore any water damage they cause.

### Fire season smoke

The foothills above Rancho Cucamonga see wildfires, and smoke and soot can reach homes well away from the flames. Our [fire and smoke damage](/fire-smoke-damage-restoration/) team cleans smoke and soot residue. We also provide [mold remediation](/mold-remediation/) when moisture has been left too long.
