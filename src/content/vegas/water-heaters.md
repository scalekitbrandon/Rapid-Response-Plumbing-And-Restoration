When a water heater fails in Las Vegas, it usually fails early. Rapid Response provides **water heater repair in Las Vegas**: we get your hot water back and make sure a leak does not leave lasting damage.

### Hard water and hot garages

Las Vegas water carries a heavy mineral load that settles in the bottom of tank water heaters as sediment. That makes the heater work harder, causes popping and rumbling and wears parts out early. Most valley water heaters also sit in garages that get very hot in summer. Regular flushing helps, and many homeowners add a water softener.

### Repair or replace

Thermostats, elements, igniters and valves can often be replaced. A leaking or badly corroded tank needs replacement. We service gas and electric tank water heaters and tankless units and tell you honestly which option makes sense.

### When it leaks

A failed tank can release dozens of gallons into a garage, closet or utility room.
