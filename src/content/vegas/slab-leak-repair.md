Concrete slab foundations are standard across Las Vegas, Henderson and North Las Vegas. They bury your water lines where you cannot see them, and Las Vegas hard water wears on those lines year after year. Rapid Response provides **slab leak repair in Las Vegas**: we find the leak, fix it and dry out everything it reached.

### Repair options

- **Spot repair** through a small, targeted opening in the floor
- **Reroute** that bypasses the leaking section through walls or the attic
- **Repipe** if the lines are failing in several places (see [repiping](/las-vegas/repiping/))

We explain the options before any work starts.

### Do not forget the flooring

A slab leak soaks the concrete, swells laminate and wood, wicks into carpet pad and climbs into drywall. Because we are also a restoration company, we measure moisture throughout the area and dry it properly with [water mitigation and structural drying](/las-vegas/water-mitigation/). Not sure where the leak is? Start with [leak detection](/las-vegas/leak-detection/).
