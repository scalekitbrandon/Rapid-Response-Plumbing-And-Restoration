Most plumbing problems start small. In Las Vegas, hard water makes them start sooner. Rapid Response handles everyday **plumbing repair in Las Vegas**: toilets, faucets, shower valves, supply lines, shut-off valves, appliance hookups and under-sink leaks.

### Hard water is hard on fixtures

The minerals in Las Vegas water leave scale inside faucets, toilet valves and shut-offs. Cartridges and seals wear out early, valves seize and supply lines degrade. Replacing worn parts before they fail is much cheaper than cleaning up a leak afterward.

### We look past the leak

Because Rapid Response is also a restoration company, we check cabinet floors, subfloor and nearby walls with a moisture meter after every repair. If water has spread, we can dry it out before it turns into [mold](/las-vegas/mold-remediation/). For leaks you cannot see, see [leak detection](/las-vegas/leak-detection/).
