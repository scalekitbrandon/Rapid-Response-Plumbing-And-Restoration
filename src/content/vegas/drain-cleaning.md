A slow drain is easy to ignore until it stops completely or a clogged main line pushes wastewater back into the house. Rapid Response provides **drain cleaning in Las Vegas**, from single fixtures to main lines, and we clean up after backups too.

### What clogs Las Vegas drains

- **Grease and food** in kitchen drains
- **Hair and soap** in showers and tubs
- **Wipes and paper products** flushed down toilets
- **Hard water scale** that narrows lines over time and catches everything else

### When the clog keeps coming back

If a main line clogs again weeks after it was cleared, the pipe itself may be cracked, sagging or full of roots. See [sewer line repair](/las-vegas/sewer-line-repair/). If wastewater has reached your floors, our [sewage cleanup](/las-vegas/sewage-cleanup/) team handles removal, sanitizing and drying.
