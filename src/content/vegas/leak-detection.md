A leak you cannot see can do more damage than one you can. Rapid Response provides **leak detection in Las Vegas**, finding the source quickly so you stop paying for wasted water and stop the damage before it spreads.

### Where Las Vegas leaks hide

- **Hot water lines under the slab**, which can warm tile and raise your bill
- **Supply lines inside walls** behind showers, tubs and toilets
- **Water heater connections** in garages and closets
- **AC condensate lines and drain pans** in attics and closets, a uniquely common Las Vegas leak
- **Ceilings below upstairs bathrooms** in two-story homes and condos

### Find it, fix it, dry it

Most leak detection companies stop at finding the leak. We repair it and deal with the water it left behind, mapping moisture in nearby walls and floors and drying it properly.
