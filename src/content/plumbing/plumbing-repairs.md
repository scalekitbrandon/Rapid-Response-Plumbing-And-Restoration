Most plumbing problems start small: a toilet that runs, a faucet that drips, a valve that sticks, a supply line that is starting to bulge. Left alone, those small problems waste water and can turn into leaks that soak floors and walls. Rapid Response handles everyday **plumbing repair in Victorville** and across the High Desert, and we look for the water damage other plumbers walk past.

### The repairs we see most

- **Toilets:** running toilets, weak flushes, leaks at the base and loose or rocking toilets
- **Faucets and fixtures:** dripping faucets, leaking shower valves, worn cartridges and loose fixtures
- **Supply lines:** aging braided lines under sinks and toilets, one of the most common causes of sudden indoor floods
- **Shut-off valves:** main and fixture valves that are stuck, leaking or will not fully close
- **Appliance connections:** dishwashers, washing machines, ice makers and water heaters
- **Under-sink leaks:** drain traps, garbage disposals and fittings that drip into the cabinet

### Hard water is hard on fixtures

Much of the High Desert has hard water. The minerals build up inside faucets, valves and fixtures, wear out cartridges and seals early and leave scale behind. If your fixtures need frequent repairs, hard water is a likely reason, and replacing worn parts before they fail is cheaper than fixing a leak afterward.

### We look past the leak

Because Rapid Response is also a restoration company, we do not just fix the drip and leave. We check the cabinet floor, subfloor and nearby walls with a moisture meter, and if water has spread, we can dry it out before it turns into mold. If you suspect a leak you cannot see, our [leak detection](/plumbing/leak-detection/) service will find it. For bigger problems, see [burst pipe repair](/plumbing/burst-pipe-repair/), [water heaters](/plumbing/water-heaters/) and [drain cleaning](/plumbing/drain-cleaning/).
