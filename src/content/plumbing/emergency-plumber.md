### What counts as a plumbing emergency?

If water is actively leaking, flooding a room or backing up into your home, it is an emergency. Common calls include burst or split pipes, leaking water heaters, slab leaks, sewer backups and toilets or fixtures that will not stop overflowing. If you are not sure, call anyway. We will help you decide what needs to happen now and what can wait.

### Why fast emergency plumbing matters

Water does not stay where it starts. Within minutes it runs under flooring, wicks up drywall and drips into the rooms below. Within a day or two, wet materials can start growing mold. The faster the water is shut off and the leak is repaired, the less you have to tear out and replace. That is why we answer around the clock and keep crews ready to roll.
