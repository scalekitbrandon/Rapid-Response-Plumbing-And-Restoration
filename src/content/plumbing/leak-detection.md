A leak you cannot see can do more damage than one you can. Water escaping inside a wall or under a floor keeps soaking into building materials day after day, often for weeks before anyone notices. Our **leak detection in Victorville** and across the High Desert finds the source quickly, so you can stop paying for wasted water and stop the damage before it grows.

### Where hidden leaks usually hide

In High Desert homes, we most often find leaks in:

- **Supply lines inside walls** behind showers, tubs, sinks and toilets
- **Hot water lines under the slab**, which can cause warm spots in the floor
- **Water heater connections** and relief valve lines in garages and closets
- **Appliance lines** for dishwashers, refrigerators with ice makers and washing machines
- **Ceilings below bathrooms** in two-story homes

### Why you should not wait on a small leak

A slow leak rarely stays small. It weakens drywall and subfloor, rots wood framing, and creates the steady moisture mold needs to grow. Many insurance policies also treat long-term leaks differently than sudden ones, so finding a leak early can matter for your claim. If you suspect a leak, it is cheaper to find it now than to rebuild later.
