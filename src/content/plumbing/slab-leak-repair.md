Concrete slab foundations are the norm across Victorville, Hesperia, Apple Valley and the rest of the High Desert. They are solid and affordable, but they also bury your water lines where you cannot see them. When one of those lines starts leaking, water spreads under your flooring for days or weeks before the signs show up. Our **slab leak repair in Victorville** finds the leak, fixes it and dries out everything it reached.

### Why slab leaks happen

Water lines under a slab sit in contact with concrete and soil for decades. Over time, hard water, ground movement and normal wear thin the pipe walls until a pinhole leak forms. Hot water lines tend to fail first because heat and pressure speed up that wear. Older High Desert homes with original copper lines are especially prone to slab leaks.

### Repair options for a slab leak

There is no one-size-fits-all fix. Depending on where the leak is and the condition of the rest of your plumbing, the right repair may be:

- **A spot repair** at the leak through a small, targeted opening in the floor
- **A reroute** that bypasses the leaking section and runs a new line through walls or the attic
- **A partial or whole-house repipe** if the lines are failing in multiple places (see [repiping](/plumbing/repiping/))

We explain the options and what each involves before any work begins.

### Do not forget the water damage

A slab leak does not just waste water. It soaks the concrete, wicks into carpet pad, swells laminate and wood flooring and climbs into the bottom of drywall. Left wet, those materials can warp and grow mold. Because Rapid Response is also a [water damage restoration](/water-damage-restoration/) company, we measure moisture throughout the affected area, dry it properly and document it for your insurance carrier.
