A burst pipe is one of the fastest ways to flood a home. A split half-inch supply line can release several gallons a minute, and it rarely happens at a good time. Rapid Response provides **burst pipe repair in Victorville** and across the High Desert 24/7. We stop the water, fix the pipe and handle the water damage it left behind.

### Why pipes burst in the High Desert

People do not expect frozen pipes in the desert, but High Desert winter nights regularly drop below freezing. Water left in exposed pipes freezes, expands and splits the pipe. Often the leak does not show until the pipe thaws the next morning, and by then water is running inside a wall or ceiling. Freezing is not the only cause. We also see bursts from:

- **Corroded galvanized and copper lines** in older Victorville, Hesperia and Apple Valley homes
- **High water pressure** that strains pipes and fittings
- **Failed braided supply lines** under sinks and toilets and behind washing machines
- **Water heater connections** and relief lines

### Repair the pipe, then deal with the water

Once the water is off, we find the break and check the lines around it, since a freeze or corrosion problem often affects more than one spot. We repair the damaged section or fitting and pressure-test the line before turning the water back on. If the pipes are failing in more than one place, we will talk you through [repiping](/plumbing/repiping/) options.

### Do not let the water damage sit

The repair is only half the job. Water from a burst pipe spreads under flooring, wicks up drywall and drips into the rooms below. Because we are also a restoration company, we start [water mitigation and structural drying](/water-mitigation/) right away, then handle any [water damage restoration](/water-damage-restoration/) the home needs. If the water is still running and you cannot find the shut-off, call our [24/7 emergency plumber](/plumbing/emergency-plumber/) line.
