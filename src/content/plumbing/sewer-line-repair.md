### What damages sewer lines

Sewer lines run from your home to the city main or septic system, often under the slab and the yard. Over the years, they fail for a few common reasons:

- **Root intrusion:** roots find small cracks and joints and grow inside the pipe
- **Age and corrosion:** older cast iron and clay lines crack, scale and break down
- **Ground movement:** shifting soil causes pipes to sag, separate or crack
- **Grease and debris:** buildup that narrows the pipe and holds back waste

### Repair or replace

Once we know where the damage is and what caused it, we explain the options. A single damaged section can often be repaired. A line with damage along its length may need to be replaced. We walk you through what each involves before any work starts. If the line is clogged but still sound, [drain cleaning](/plumbing/drain-cleaning/) may be all you need.

### When sewage gets into the home

A failed sewer line often pushes wastewater up through tubs, floor drains and toilets. That is a health hazard, and household cleaning is not enough.
