### Hard water and High Desert water heaters

Water across much of the High Desert is hard, meaning it carries a lot of dissolved minerals. Those minerals settle in the bottom of tank water heaters as sediment. Over time, sediment makes the heater less efficient, causes popping and rumbling noises, and wears out parts and the tank itself sooner. Flushing a tank on a regular schedule helps it last longer.

### Repair or replacement

Not every water heater problem means you need a new unit. Thermostats, heating elements, igniters, gas valves and other parts can often be replaced. But when the tank itself is leaking or badly corroded, replacement is the only real fix. We will tell you honestly which makes more sense for your situation before we start. We work on:

- **Gas and electric tank water heaters**
- **Tankless water heaters**
- **Supply lines, valves and connections** that leak or fail

### When a water heater leaks

Water heaters often sit in garages, hall closets or interior utility rooms. A slow leak can soak into drywall, baseboards and flooring for weeks, and a failed tank can release dozens of gallons at once. Because Rapid Response is also a [water damage restoration](/water-damage-restoration/) company, we check the surrounding area with moisture meters, remove materials that cannot be saved and dry everything properly.
