Las Vegas gets little rain, but monsoon storms can drop a lot of it in a short time, and hard desert ground sends it straight into garages, yards and homes. Inside, burst lines and failed water heaters flood properties year-round. Rapid Response provides **flood damage restoration in Las Vegas** around the clock.

### Clean, gray and contaminated water

Not all flood water is the same. Clean water from a supply line can often be dried in place. Storm runoff and sewage are contaminated, and porous materials they touch usually need to be removed. We identify the type of water first so the cleanup is safe and done right.

### More than a wet floor

Flood water travels under flooring, behind baseboards and into drywall. We extract it, remove what cannot be saved, sanitize where needed and run [structural drying](/las-vegas/water-mitigation/) until readings confirm the structure is dry. If sewage was involved, see [sewage cleanup](/las-vegas/sewage-cleanup/).
