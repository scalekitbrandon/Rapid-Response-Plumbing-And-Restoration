When a pipe bursts or water starts coming through the ceiling, you need an **emergency plumber in Las Vegas** who answers the phone and gets moving. Rapid Response is on call 24/7 across the Las Vegas Valley for homes, condos, rentals and businesses.

### Why Las Vegas plumbing fails

Las Vegas water is some of the hardest in the country. The minerals build up inside water heaters, valves and fixtures, wear out seals and fittings early, and narrow older pipes. Add garages that bake in summer heat and slab foundations that hide water lines, and you get the emergencies we see every week: burst water heaters, slab leaks, split supply lines and main line backups.

### Fix the plumbing, then handle the damage

Most plumbers fix the pipe and leave. Rapid Response is also a restoration company, so once the leak is repaired we can start [water mitigation and structural drying](/las-vegas/water-mitigation/) right away and handle any [water damage restoration](/las-vegas/water-damage-restoration/) your property needs. If sewage backed up, our [sewage cleanup](/las-vegas/sewage-cleanup/) team takes care of it.

### Condos, rentals and businesses

In condos and multi-unit buildings, an emergency in one unit quickly affects the unit below. We work with owners, HOAs, landlords and property managers and document the work clearly for everyone involved.
