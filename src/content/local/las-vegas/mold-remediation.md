Many people assume mold is not a problem in Las Vegas. But mold needs moisture, not humidity, and a slow leak, an AC condensate overflow or water damage that was never fully dried gives it everything it needs. Rapid Response provides **mold remediation in Las Vegas**, and because we are also a plumbing company, we fix the moisture source.

### The Las Vegas AC factor

Air conditioners run for months here and produce a lot of condensate. When a line clogs or a drain pan cracks, water drips into ceilings, closets and walls, often for weeks before anyone notices. That is one of the most common mold sources we see in the valley.

### What remediation includes

Inspection, containment, removal of affected materials, cleaning of remaining surfaces and moisture control so the conditions that grew the mold are gone.

### Fix the source

Cleaning without fixing the moisture means the mold comes back. Our plumbers find hidden leaks with [leak detection](/las-vegas/leak-detection/), and our restoration team dries the area with [structural drying](/las-vegas/water-mitigation/).
