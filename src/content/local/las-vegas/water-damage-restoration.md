Water damage does not wait, even in the desert. Rapid Response provides **water damage restoration in Las Vegas** 24/7, from extraction and drying to removing what cannot be saved and documenting everything for your insurance claim.

### Las Vegas water damage is different

Dry air does not dry a building. Water that gets into subfloor, framing and wall cavities stays trapped inside an air-conditioned home. Las Vegas also has its own common causes: **AC condensate overflows**, **water heaters worn out by hard water**, **slab leaks** and **condo leaks** that travel from one unit to the next.

### What restoration includes

- Stopping the source, including repairing the plumbing that failed
- Emergency extraction and [structural drying](/las-vegas/water-mitigation/)
- Removing drywall, flooring and insulation that cannot be saved
- Moisture readings and photo documentation for your claim
