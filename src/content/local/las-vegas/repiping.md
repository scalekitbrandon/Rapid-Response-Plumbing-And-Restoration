If you have called a plumber for leak after leak, the problem is probably not bad luck. Rapid Response provides **repiping in Las Vegas**, replacing failing supply lines so you can stop patching leaks.

### Polybutylene and aging pipes

A lot of Las Vegas was built during the boom years of the late 1970s through the mid 1990s, when polybutylene was a common supply pipe. It is known for failing suddenly. Copper lines in older homes also develop pinhole leaks from hard water and corrosion.

### Partial or whole house

If the problems are limited to one area, such as the lines under the slab, a partial repipe or reroute may be right. If leaks are showing up throughout the home, a whole-house repipe usually gives the best value. For a single active leak, start with [leak detection](/las-vegas/leak-detection/) or [slab leak repair](/las-vegas/slab-leak-repair/).

### New pipes, no leftover damage

Homes that need repiping have usually had leaks, and those leaks often left moisture and mold in walls. Because we are also a restoration company, we check opened walls and dry and restore old damage before closing them up. See [water damage restoration](/las-vegas/water-damage-restoration/).
