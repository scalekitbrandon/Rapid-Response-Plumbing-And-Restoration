A burst pipe is one of the fastest ways to flood a home. Rapid Response provides **burst pipe repair in Las Vegas** 24/7: we stop the water, fix the pipe and handle the water damage it left behind.

### What makes Las Vegas pipes burst

Freezing is a smaller factor here than in colder places, but it does happen during cold snaps, especially in higher parts of the valley. More often, Las Vegas bursts come from **high water pressure**, **hard water corrosion**, **failed braided supply lines** and **aging pipe materials**. Many homes built from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s were plumbed with polybutylene, a gray plastic pipe known for failing without warning.

### Repair the pipe, check the rest

Once the water is off, we find the break and check the lines around it. If the pipes are failing in more than one place, especially polybutylene or corroded copper, we will explain your [repiping](/las-vegas/repiping/) options instead of patching leak after leak.

### Then dry it out

Water from a burst pipe spreads under flooring and into walls fast. Because we are also a restoration company, we start [water mitigation and structural drying](/las-vegas/water-mitigation/) on the same visit. If water is still running, call our [24/7 emergency plumber](/las-vegas/emergency-plumber/) line.
