If the main line has been cleared more than once and the backups keep coming back, cleaning is not the answer. Rapid Response provides **sewer line repair in Las Vegas**, and because we are also a restoration company, we clean up the sewage a failed line leaves behind.

### What damages Las Vegas sewer lines

- **Roots** from landscaping trees and shrubs that grow into joints
- **Ground movement** that cracks, separates or sags pipe
- **Grease and debris** that narrow the line
- **Age**, especially in older central Las Vegas neighborhoods

### Repair or replace

Once we know where the damage is, we explain the options, from repairing a section to replacing the line. If the line is sound but clogged, [drain cleaning](/las-vegas/drain-cleaning/) may be enough. If sewage reached your floors, our [sewage cleanup](/las-vegas/sewage-cleanup/) team handles it.
