When water gets loose in a Las Vegas home or business, the clock starts. **Water mitigation** is the work that stops the spread: extraction, removing what cannot be saved and drying the structure. Rapid Response provides water mitigation and structural drying in Las Vegas 24/7.

### Dry air is not enough

Las Vegas has low humidity, which helps drying, but it does not dry the inside of a wall. Water trapped in subfloor, framing, cabinets and insulation stays put without targeted airflow and dehumidification. We measure moisture from day one and keep drying until the readings, not the surface, say the structure is dry.

### Mitigation vs. restoration

Mitigation keeps the damage from getting worse. [Water damage restoration](/las-vegas/water-damage-restoration/) covers the full job, including repairs. Insurance often handles the two separately.
