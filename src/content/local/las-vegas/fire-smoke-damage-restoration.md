A fire leaves behind more than burned materials. Smoke and soot travel through the property, and the water used to put the fire out soaks into everything below it. Rapid Response provides **fire damage restoration in Las Vegas**, handling all three so your property can move toward recovery.

### Keep the AC off

In Las Vegas, the instinct is to turn the air back on. After a fire, running the HVAC can push soot through the ducts and into every room. Leave it off until it has been checked.

### Water damage comes with it

Firefighting water and sprinkler discharge soak carpet, subfloor, drywall and insulation, and in condos it often reaches the unit below. Because water damage is our specialty, we extract and dry it as part of every fire job. See [water damage restoration](/las-vegas/water-damage-restoration/).

### What fire restoration includes

Assessment, stabilization, removal of burned and unsalvageable materials, smoke and soot cleanup, water extraction and drying, and documentation for your claim. For businesses, see [commercial restoration](/las-vegas/commercial-property-restoration/).
