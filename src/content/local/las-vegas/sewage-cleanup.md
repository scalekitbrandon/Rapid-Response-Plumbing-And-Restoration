A sewage backup is one of the most unpleasant and hazardous things a property owner can face. Rapid Response provides **sewage cleanup in Las Vegas** 24/7, and because we are also a plumbing company, we fix the problem that caused it.

### Why sewage needs professional cleanup

Sewage is the most contaminated category of water damage. Porous materials it touches, like carpet, pad, drywall and insulation, usually have to be removed. Hard surfaces are cleaned and sanitized, and the structure is dried and verified with moisture readings so odors and mold do not follow.

### Condos and multi-unit buildings

In Las Vegas condos and apartments, a backup in one unit can spread to others. We work with owners, HOAs and property managers and document the cleanup clearly.

### Fix the cause

Most backups come from a clogged or damaged line. Our plumbers handle [drain cleaning](/las-vegas/drain-cleaning/) and [sewer line repair](/las-vegas/sewer-line-repair/), so the same team stops the backup and cleans up after it.
