### Cold snaps on the higher ground

Hesperia rises from about 3,200 feet near the Mojave River to well over 3,500 feet toward Oak Hills and the Cajon Pass. Clear winter nights drop below freezing fast, and pipes in garages, attics and exterior walls are the first to go. We repair the break with our [Hesperia emergency plumbers](/hesperia/emergency-plumber/) and start [water mitigation and structural drying](/hesperia/water-mitigation/) on the same visit, so the leak and the wet wall get handled by one crew.

### Septic, sewer and what comes up the drain

Large parts of Hesperia were never connected to a sewer main, and the city's own wastewater plan still maps which areas are on septic. When a tank fills or a leach field gives out, wastewater backs up into the lowest fixture in the house. That is a contaminated loss, not a clean leak, and anything porous it touched has to come out. We handle the cleanup and sanitizing, and if the main line inside the house is the problem, our plumbers clear it.

### Storm water on graded lots

Hesperia doesn't get much rain, but summer monsoon cells and the odd winter storm can drop a lot at once. On lots graded toward the house or along unpaved roads, that runoff finds the garage door and the slab edge. We extract it, pull wet materials and dry the structure, then show you where the water came in so you can regrade or add a berm.

### Before mold sets in

A warm, closed Hesperia house with wet drywall can grow mold in two to three days. If a leak sat over a weekend or behind a cabinet, see [mold remediation in Hesperia](/hesperia/mold-remediation/). We document readings and photos for your insurance claim from the first visit.
