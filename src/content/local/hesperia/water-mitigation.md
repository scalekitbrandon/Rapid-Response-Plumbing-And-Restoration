### Fast extraction matters more here than you'd think

Many Hesperia homes have particleboard: manufactured home floors, cabinet bases and older stair treads. Particleboard swells and falls apart when it stays wet. ### Freeze breaks, dried right

Most of the water losses we dry in Hesperia start with a frozen pipe. They happen in the coldest weeks of the year, when cold air in the house slows drying. We heat the work area as needed and run dehumidifiers sized to the space so the framing dries in days, not weeks.
