### Slow leaks in established homes

Many Apple Valley homes have original plumbing, and older fittings and supply lines tend to drip rather than burst. A slow leak inside a wall or cabinet can run for months, giving mold everything it needs. We contain the area, remove affected materials, clean and dry, then repair the leak.
