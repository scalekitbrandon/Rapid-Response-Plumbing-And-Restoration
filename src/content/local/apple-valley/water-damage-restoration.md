### Septic backups are part of life here

The Town's own planning documents put roughly 70% of Apple Valley's developed residential areas on septic. When a tank fills or a leach field fails, the wastewater comes back up through the lowest drain: a shower, a tub or a floor drain. That's a contaminated loss. We extract it, remove porous materials it touched, sanitize and dry, then tell you whether the line, the tank or the leach field caused it.

### Summer storms and sliding doors

Apple Valley's monsoon storms are short and heavy. On lots that slope toward the house, or that sit below dirt roads, runoff reaches patios and slides under sliding doors and garage doors. We extract the water, pull wet baseboards and pad and dry the walls. Once it's dry, we'll show you where the water came in.
