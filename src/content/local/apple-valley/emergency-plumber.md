When something breaks at night, you need an **emergency plumber in Apple Valley** who answers the phone and knows how homes here are put together. Rapid Response is on call 24/7, with crews on the way in 1 hour or less.

### Big lots mean long lines

Apple Valley has some of the largest residential lots in the Victor Valley, and many homes sit well back from the street. Long service lines can leak underground for weeks before you notice a soft spot in the yard or a bill that doubled. On horse properties, extra lines to barns and troughs add more places for a break. We find the leak, repair it and, if the line is failing in several places, quote a replacement. For leaks you can't see, see [leak detection](/plumbing/leak-detection/).

### Most of Apple Valley is on septic

The Town's own planning documents put roughly 70% of developed residential areas on septic rather than sewer. So when drains back up in Apple Valley, it's often a septic problem, not a clog. We clear the line from the house out to the tank, tell you plainly if the tank or leach field is the issue, and clean up any wastewater that reached your floors.

### Cold nights, hard water

Apple Valley doesn't get as cold as Hesperia's higher ground, but winter nights still drop below freezing. Exposed lines in garages, on exterior walls and out in the yard freeze and split. The groundwater here is hard, which builds scale in water heaters and valves. We repair freeze breaks and handle [water heater repair and replacement](/plumbing/water-heaters/) on the same visit.
