When a pipe bursts or the sewer backs up in San Bernardino, you need a plumber who answers and understands older houses. Rapid Response is the **emergency plumber in San Bernardino** that's on call 24/7 for homes, rentals and businesses.

### Old pipe under old houses

Some of San Bernardino's homes go back to the early 1900s, and many neighborhoods near downtown were built before 1950. Plenty of them still have galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron or clay drains. Galvanized pipe rusts from the inside out until it bursts, often under a raised floor or inside a wall. We repair the failure, check the rest of the run and give you a straight answer on whether [repiping](/plumbing/repiping/) makes more sense than another patch.

### Roots and sewer laterals

Mature trees line a lot of San Bernardino's older streets, and their roots find the joints in old clay sewer laterals. The result is a main line backup, usually when everyone's using water in the morning or evening. We clear the line and, if it keeps happening, explain repair and replacement options. See [sewer line repair](/plumbing/sewer-line-repair/) for more.

### Rentals and multi-unit buildings

San Bernardino has a lot of rental homes, duplexes and apartment buildings. A plumbing failure in one unit often affects others. We work with tenants, owners and property managers, get access to every affected unit and document the repair for everyone.
