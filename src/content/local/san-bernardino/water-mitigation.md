Water mitigation is the emergency part of a water loss: getting the water out and stopping the damage before it spreads. Rapid Response provides **water mitigation and structural drying in San Bernardino**, 24/7, for homes, rentals and commercial buildings.

### Under the floor, not just on it

A lot of San Bernardino homes, especially older ones near downtown, have raised floors over a crawl space. When water goes through the floor, it collects underneath. Drying the carpet doesn't dry the joists or the subfloor. We get under the house, extract the water and set up drying from below, then monitor both levels until they're dry.

### Heat speeds up mold

San Bernardino gets hot in summer, and a warm, closed house with wet drywall is the right environment for mold to start within days. That's why we set up [water extraction](/water-extraction/) and drying on the first visit, not the next day. If a leak has already sat for a while, see [mold remediation in San Bernardino](/san-bernardino/mold-remediation/).

### Fix it, dry it, document it

If the water is still coming, our [San Bernardino emergency plumbers](/san-bernardino/emergency-plumber/) repair the source first. Every job gets a moisture map, daily readings and photos so your insurance adjuster can see what got wet and when it was dry. When drying is complete, [water damage restoration in San Bernardino](/san-bernardino/water-damage-restoration/) covers the repairs.
