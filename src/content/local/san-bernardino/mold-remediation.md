Mold in San Bernardino often starts somewhere you can't see: under a raised floor, inside a wall behind an old tile shower or in a rental where a leak went unreported. Rapid Response provides **mold remediation in San Bernardino** that removes the growth and fixes the moisture behind it.

### Crawl spaces are the hidden problem

Many older San Bernardino homes have raised floors over a crawl space. A slow drain leak, a supply line drip or storm water that gets under the house keeps the crawl space damp, and mold grows on the joists and subfloor. Often the first sign is a musty smell coming up through the floor. We inspect the crawl space, remove the affected material and dry it out.
